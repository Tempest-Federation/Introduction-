Welcome to Tempest Federation

Tempest Federation is an anime-based community created for people who enjoy anime, games, conversations and simply having a good time together.

The server has a slightly mature atmosphere, but one thing stays important — everyone is treated equally. Different personalities, different interests and different people can all have their own place here.
