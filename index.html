I’m Kitaryo, the creator of Tempest Federation. I’m a shy and introverted person who likes keeping life simple. I don’t really have a favourite colour, food or many favourite things — anime is the exception. I mostly play chess and enjoy having my own space.

My favourite anime is Dragon Ball. I created this server because I had a dream of having a federation of my own, inspired by the idea of Rimuru Tempest and his federation. Tempest Federation is my way of turning that idea into a real community where people can have fun together.
